Run:
openssl ts -verify -data forecast-2026-11-02-snapshot.zip -in forecast.tsr -CAfile digicert-root.pem -untrusted timestamp-chain.pem

Expected result: Verification: OK

To inspect the signed time:
openssl ts -reply -in forecast.tsr -text

The timestamp authenticates the file fingerprint and the authority's time assertion. It does not certify forecast accuracy or authorship.
